Nevermore Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 6, 2026

Nevermore is a Chrome extension that visualizes how much time remains across multiple scales of time. It can display the percentage remaining in the minute, hour, day, week, month, year, and—optionally—life, based on a user-entered birthday and expected lifespan.

Information Nevermore uses

If you configure the life gauge, Nevermore stores your birthdate, expected lifespan, and preferred display mode.

Where the information is stored

Nevermore uses the Chrome Storage Sync API so these settings can follow you across Chrome instances where sync is enabled. Nevermore does not operate a server that receives these settings.

Sharing, analytics, and advertising

Nevermore does not sell, share, or transfer your settings to advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties. Nevermore does not use analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, or browsing-history access.

Permissions

storage — used only to save your optional life-gauge settings and display preference.
